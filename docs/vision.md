# What we are building

From the leadership discussion.

## Flow
1. Leader creates a project: goal and what the product should do. On submit they tag owners and pick departments (AI, Development, Operations). Start date and time is set by the system.
2. Tagged people get notified and there is one meeting to check if it is doable. Each lead says yes or no. All yes moves it forward.
3. Backlog gets created: AI work, data work, operations work, and external things like BSNL.
4. Owners add their own time estimates per item, and the total gives the end date. Each item also gets a weightage (%).
5. Leads break items into tasks and assign people. If someone is blocked on another person they tag them as a dependency.
6. Testing does the same thing: scope it, estimate it, feedback loop, review meeting.

## What the dashboard has to answer
- Where are we against the main goals, what is moving, stuck or at risk
- Who is contributing, who is blocking whom and why
- Which milestones are at risk, who is overloaded or free
- What got done yesterday, what is due today
- Where does the leader / head need to step in
- How far are we from the goal

## Other things said in the meeting
- Planning properly for the first two days is on purpose, it saves months later
- Cards should visibly move between levels and raise an alarm when stuck
- Drag and drop board like Zoho
- Use RACI for who is responsible / accountable / informed
- Cliq integration comes later, get the core working first
